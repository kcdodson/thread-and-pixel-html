§ 06 — Common questions

What Chattanooga business owners ask us.

How much does a website cost in Chattanooga?

Most small business websites we build range from $1,500 to $6,000 depending on size and complexity. A clean 5-page site with a contact form typically runs $1,500–$2,500. E-commerce, booking systems, or custom features push that higher. We give you a clear, fixed quote before any work begins — no hourly surprises.

How long does a web design project take?

A typical small business site takes 3–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. We start with a discovery call to understand your business and goals, move into design and your review, then build and test everything before going live. Rush timelines are available for an additional fee.

Do I need a website if I already have a Facebook page?

Yes — and here's why it matters: you don't own your Facebook audience. If the algorithm changes or your account gets restricted, you can lose access to your followers overnight. Your website is a permanent asset you control. It also shows up in Google search results, which Facebook pages rarely do for local service queries.

What is local SEO and why does it matter for my Chattanooga business?

Local SEO is the practice of optimizing your online presence so you appear when people nearby search for what you offer. That means showing up in the Google map pack, ranking in local search results, and having a well-optimized Google Business Profile. Most people searching "plumber near me" or "web design Chattanooga" click one of the first three results — local SEO is how you get there.

Do you work with businesses outside Chattanooga?

Our focus is the greater Chattanooga metro area — including North Shore, Southside, East Brainerd, Hixson, Red Bank, Signal Mountain, and nearby cities like Cleveland, TN and Dalton, GA. We occasionally take on projects further afield when it's a great fit, so feel free to reach out.
