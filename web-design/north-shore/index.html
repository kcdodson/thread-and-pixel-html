About the area

North Shore well.

The North Shore is one of Chattanooga's most distinctive neighborhoods — a walkable stretch along the Tennessee River known for its creative energy, independent businesses, and a customer base that does its research before it buys.

North Shore customers find businesses online. Whether you're a yoga studio on Manufacturers Road, a restaurant near Coolidge Park, or a boutique on Frazier Avenue, your website is often the first impression you make. We build websites that match the quality of the neighborhood you're in.

As a Chattanooga-based studio, we've worked with businesses up and down the North Shore. We understand the local customer, the local search landscape, and what it takes to compete here.
