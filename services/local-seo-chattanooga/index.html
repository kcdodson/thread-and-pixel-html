Common questions

Local SEO questions
Chattanooga businesses ask

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of optimizing your online presence so your business appears when people nearby search for what you offer — in Google Maps, the local pack, and organic results. It involves your Google Business Profile, your website content, local citations, and reviews.

How long does local SEO take to show results?

Most businesses start seeing movement in local rankings within 60–90 days. Google Business Profile optimizations can show results faster — sometimes within a few weeks. Full results typically take 4–6 months of consistent work.

Do I need local SEO if I already have a website?

Having a website is not the same as ranking locally. Local SEO ensures your business appears in Google Maps, the local pack, and "near me" searches. Most websites without active local SEO work are invisible to nearby customers looking to buy.

How important are Google reviews for local rankings?

Very important. Google uses review count, review recency, and average rating as ranking signals for the local pack. We help you build a consistent review acquisition process so your profile stays competitive — without paying for fake reviews.

Do you offer ongoing local SEO, or just a one-time setup?

Both. We can set up your local SEO foundations as a one-time project, or manage your local presence on an ongoing monthly basis — keeping your GBP active, monitoring rankings, and building new citations over time.
