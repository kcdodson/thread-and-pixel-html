Common questions

Web design questions
Chattanooga businesses ask us

How much does web design cost in Chattanooga?

Most small business websites range from $1,500 to $6,000. A 5-page site with a contact form typically runs $1,500–$2,500. E-commerce or custom features push higher. We quote every project with a fixed price up front — no hourly surprises.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects go from kickoff to launch in 3–6 weeks. That includes a discovery call, the design phase, your review round, full development, and final testing before we go live.

Do you build websites for specific industries?

Yes — we've built sites for restaurants, contractors, retail shops, medical practices, professional services, salons, and more. Every site is custom-built for your specific business, not from a template.

Will my website work on mobile?

Every site we build is designed mobile-first. We design for phones before desktop, because over 60% of local Chattanooga searches happen on a mobile device.

Can I update my website myself after it's built?

Yes — we build sites on platforms you can manage, and we train you on how to make common updates. For anything more complex, our maintenance plans include monthly updates as part of the package.
