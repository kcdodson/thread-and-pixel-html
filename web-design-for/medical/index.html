Common questions

Medical website
questions we hear often

Are medical websites subject to HIPAA?

Your website itself isn't subject to HIPAA, but any contact forms or patient intake tools that collect health information are. We build forms that don't store PHI on our servers and recommend HIPAA-compliant third-party tools for patient intake.

How do patients find a new doctor online?

Primarily through Google search and Google Maps. A well-optimized Google Business Profile with reviews, combined with a fast website that lists your services, providers, and insurance, is the combination that drives new patient inquiries.

Can you integrate with our existing EHR or scheduling software?

Yes — we can link to most scheduling platforms (Zocdoc, Healow, Athena, etc.) or build a custom booking form. We'll work with whatever system you already use.
