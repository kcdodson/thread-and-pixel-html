Common questions

Restaurant website
questions we hear often

What should a restaurant website include?

At minimum: your full menu with prices, hours, location with a map embed, phone number, and a way to make reservations or place orders. We also optimize for Google restaurant search features like menus in search results and food ordering integrations.

How do I get my restaurant to show up in Google?

The biggest factors are your Google Business Profile (with menu, photos, and regular updates), local SEO on your website, and consistent reviews. We handle all three as part of every restaurant website project.

Can my customers order online through my website?

Yes — we can integrate online ordering directly into your site or connect to third-party platforms like Toast, Square, or ChowNow. We'll recommend the best option for your restaurant's setup and volume.

How much does a restaurant website cost in Chattanooga?

Most restaurant websites range from $2,000 to $5,000 depending on menu complexity, photography needs, and whether you need ordering integration. We give a fixed quote up front — no hourly billing.
