Why it matters

Local retailers win online
or lose to chains.

Independent Chattanooga retailers compete with national chains on Google. When someone searches "home decor store Chattanooga" or "women's boutique North Shore," the results are whoever has the best-optimized web presence — and national chains invest heavily in that. The stores winning local search have fast websites with great product photography, clear hours, and strong local SEO.

We build all of that. We've worked with North Shore boutiques, Southside shops, and independent retailers across the city. We know the Chattanooga shopper, what they search for, and what makes them choose a local store over Amazon or a chain. Your website should reflect the quality of what you sell and where you sell it.

Common questions

Retail website
questions we hear often

Do I need an online store, or just a website?

Not every retailer needs e-commerce. A well-optimized local website that showcases your products, hours, and location often drives more in-store foot traffic than a full online store. We'll help you decide what's right for your business.

How do I get my Chattanooga store to show up in Google?

Your Google Business Profile and website need to work together. We optimize both — your GBP with photos and products, and your website with local keywords and schema markup.

Can you help with product photography recommendations?

We don't shoot photography ourselves, but we work with local Chattanooga photographers and can connect you with someone. We'll tell you exactly what shots you need for the best website results.
