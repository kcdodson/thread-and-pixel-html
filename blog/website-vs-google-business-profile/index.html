Website vs. Google Business Profile: What Does Your Chattanooga Business Actually Need?

June 16, 2026 · 6 min read

A common question from Chattanooga business owners: "I'm already showing up on Google Maps — do I still need a website?" It's fair. Some businesses genuinely rank in the map pack with a minimal web presence. But the answer is almost always the same: you need both, and they do very different things.

What your Google Business Profile actually does

Your GBP is what powers the local pack — the three map results that appear at the top of Google when someone searches "plumber Chattanooga" or "coffee shop near me." It's free, managed directly in Google, and for local searches it often gets more clicks than the organic results below it.

A well-optimized GBP shows your hours, photos, reviews, phone number, and sometimes your menu or service list — all without a visitor ever needing to reach your website. For a lot of Chattanooga customers, that information is enough to call you.

What your GBP can't do

For all its power, GBP has real limits that matter for any business trying to grow.

"You don't own your Google Business Profile. Google controls the platform. Listings get suspended — sometimes without explanation. Competitors can suggest edits to your listing. Google can change what's displayed and how it looks. You have far less control than it might seem."

GBP also doesn't rank for informational searches. "How often should I get my teeth cleaned" or "best dentist in Chattanooga" — those queries send traffic to websites with real content, not to GBP listings. If you only have a GBP, you're invisible to everyone who isn't already searching specifically for what you do.

And for higher-ticket services — contractors, lawyers, accountants, web designers — customers want to see more before they commit to a call. A GBP listing gives them basics. A real website gives them your story, your work, your process, your pricing, and a reason to trust you over the competition.

What your website does that GBP can't

  • Ranks for keywords that GBP never touches — informational, comparison, and long-tail searches
  • Tells your full brand story with images, copy, and real testimonials
  • Captures leads through contact forms, chat, or email
  • Works as a landing page for paid advertising
  • Is a permanent asset you fully own and control

The businesses winning Chattanooga local search use both

The businesses consistently appearing at the top of Google in Chattanooga have a fully optimized GBP and a fast, well-built website. The GBP wins them the map pack for high-intent local searches. The website wins them everything else — and handles the actual converting once someone arrives.

If you only have a GBP, you're leaving organic rankings on the table. If you only have a website with no GBP, you're invisible in the map pack. You need both, and they need to point at each other — your GBP should link to your website, and your website should reinforce the same business name, phone number, and location as your GBP.

How to prioritize if you're starting from zero

If you have neither, start with your GBP — it's free and can start driving calls within days of being claimed and properly set up. Then build the website. If you have a GBP but no website, get the website — especially if you're in a service business where customers research before buying. If you have a website but no GBP, claim and optimize it today; it's free and it's one of the highest-ROI things you can do.

Common questions

Can I rank in Google Maps without a website?
Yes, some businesses do. But it's harder, and your ranking ceiling is lower. Google uses your website as a trust signal for local pack rankings. A well-optimized website helps your GBP rank higher.
My GBP links to an old website — does that matter?
Yes, significantly. The website URL on your GBP should point to a fast, current, well-optimized site. An outdated or slow site linked from your GBP is a negative signal for local rankings.
How do I get more Google reviews for my Chattanooga business?
Ask right after a completed job, personally and directly. A text message with a direct link to your review page converts best. Don't offer incentives — Google prohibits it, and customers can often tell.
